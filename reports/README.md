# Archived workstream reports

These are the per-workstream reports and harness result tables produced while bringing this fork up to
modern WebAssembly (see the summary in `MERGE_NOTES.md` and the roadmap in `MODERN_WASM_GAPS.md` on
`main`). They were moved off `main` because they are development history, not part of the plugin - the
scripts on `main` regenerate every result table:

| archived file | regenerated by (on `main`) |
| --- | --- |
| `REPORT-<WS>.md` (one per workstream) | `test-scripts/check-local.ps1`, `run_probes.ps1`, `test-scripts/spectest/run_spectest.ps1`, `corpus-sweep.ps1`, `robustness-sweep.ps1` - the reports describe what was run and what changed |
| `DIAGNOSIS.md` | the component-model investigation (superseded by `REPORT-C2.md`) |
| `spectest-BASELINE.md` | `test-scripts/spectest/run_spectest.ps1` (`core`/`all` subsets) |
| `spectest-NEGATIVE.md` | `test-scripts/spectest/run_spectest_negative.ps1` (2948-module table) |
| `corpus-RESULTS.md` | `test-scripts/corpus-sweep.ps1` |
| `robustness-RESULTS.md` | `test-scripts/robustness-sweep.ps1` |

The authoritative machine-checked expectations stay on `main` as data, not prose:
`test-scripts/expected-results.json` (136-probe gate) and `test-scripts/spectest-expected.json`
(spec-suite gate).
## Research reports (`reports/research/`)

External-tool reuse analyses produced for the plugin (what could be ported from other WebAssembly
projects, with licensing and experiments):

* `notdec-reuse-analysis.md` - NotDec (github.com/NotDec/NotDec) + arXiv 2608.03286v1, plus a WABT
  `wasm-objdump` decode comparison against our listing.
* `binaryen-reuse-analysis.md` - WebAssembly/binaryen (`wasm-dis` validity oracle, decode rules, test
  corpus, and what not to port).

The actionable summary lives on `main` in `REUSE_OPPORTUNITIES.md`.
